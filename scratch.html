Mastering the Craft: The Art of Piano

My love for the piano began before I could even play it. My father, who doubled as my teacher, is a skilled pianist. He has played for as long as I can remember, accustoming my ears to the sounds of the keys and the associated beauty.

By the time kindgergarten rolled around, I was eager to take my place on the piano bench. My patient father showed me the ropes, teaching me how to read music and understand rhythm. He had a piano lesson with me every single day for the next 8 years. It was not easy, but I slowly grew into a talented pianist myself.

Piano has become one of the most significant parts of my life. The development of this talent has blessed my life and given me opportunities to bless others. It is a form of both expression and escape.
